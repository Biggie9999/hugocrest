FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the process for applying for a loan or mortgage with HugoCrest Bank?

To apply for a loan or mortgage, you can visit our website to fill out an online application, or you can contact your relationship manager or visit a HugoCrest Bank branch for personalized assistance. Our team will guide you through the application process and help you find the best financing options for your needs.

What types of accounts can be linked for Overdraft Protection Sweeps?

You can link multiple types of accounts, including savings, checking, or other business accounts, to provide a robust safety net against overdrafts.

How do I enroll in online and mobile banking?

Enrolling in online and mobile banking is simple. Visit our website or download the HugoCrest Bank mobile app, and follow the prompts to create your account. If you need assistance, our customer service team is available to help you get set up.

What should I do if I suspect fraud on my account?

If you suspect fraud on your account, immediately contact HugoCrest Bank’s customer service or your relationship manager. We will take the necessary steps to secure your account and investigate any unauthorized transactions.

Can I customize the transfer thresholds for Overdraft Protection Sweeps?

Yes, you can customize the transfer thresholds based on your business needs. Work with your relationship manager to determine the optimal settings for your account.
