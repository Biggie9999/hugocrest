How It Works

Automatic Transfers

Our system monitors your account balances in real-time. When your primary account’s balance drops below a predefined threshold, funds are automatically transferred from your linked accounts to cover the shortfall.

Customizable Thresholds

Set your own transfer thresholds based on your business needs. You can choose the minimum balance that triggers a sweep, ensuring your account is always within your comfort zone.

Linked Accounts

Link multiple accounts, including savings, checking, or other business accounts, to provide a robust safety net against overdrafts.

Notification Alerts

Receive instant notifications via email or SMS whenever a sweep is performed, keeping you informed and in control of your finances.
